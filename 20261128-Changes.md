---

## 1. Objective

Create a new **Agentic Activity Dashboard** tab powered by the **Brokers** tab's **Agentic Activity Ledger** (`/api/rh/data` & Robinhood MCP filled orders), starting with **Starting Cash Available = \$500.00**, displaying:
- **Cash Available**, **Invested Capital**, **Total Net Equity** (with dollar & percentage change since \$500.00 start) and a horizontal allocation bar
- **Realized P/L**, **Winning Percentage**, **Open at Cost**, **Orders Filled** (`buys · sells`), and **Volume Traded**
- **Realized P/L over time** (step-line chart across closed sell fills)
- **Realized P/L by symbol** (diverging horizontal bar chart sorted from largest loss to largest gain)

---

## 2. Implementation Plan

1. **Backend FIFO Analytics Service & Endpoint (`backend/services/agentic_analytics.py`, `backend/main.py`)**:
   - Create `compute_agentic_dashboard(portfolio, orders, starting_cash=500.0)` to replay all filled equity `BUY` and `SELL` orders in chronological order using **FIFO (First-In, First-Out)** lot matching per symbol.
   - Compute account summary metrics relative to `starting_cash = $500.00` (`since_start_pnl = total_equity - 500.00`, `since_start_pct`), cash/invested percentages, `open_at_cost`, `win_rate_pct` (`winning_sells / total_sells × 100`), `realized_pnl`, `realized_pnl_pct` on closed cost, `volume_traded`, `realized_pnl_over_time` step points, and `realized_pnl_by_symbol` sorted ascending.
   - Add `fetch_all_live_filled_orders()` to paginate Robinhood MCP `get_equity_orders(state="filled")` via `cursor` so full trade history beyond page 1 is captured.
   - Expose `GET /api/rh/activity-dashboard?starting_cash=500.0` in `backend/main.py`.
2. **Frontend Dashboard Tab (`frontend/src/components/AgenticActivityDashboard.tsx`, `frontend/src/app/page.tsx`)**:
   - Build `AgenticActivityDashboard.tsx` with:
     - Top bar featuring environment/account badges, editable **Start Cash (\$)** input (default `500`), `Last synced` timestamp, and **Refresh Dashboard** button.
     - **Account now** card with **Total Net Equity**, **Cash Available**, **Invested Capital**, and an **Invested vs. Cash** horizontal progress bar.
     - 5 KPI summary cards (**Realized P/L**, **Winning Percentage**, **Open at Cost**, **Orders Filled**, **Volume Traded**).
     - **Realized P/L over time** Recharts step-line chart (`type="stepAfter"`) with `$0` reference line, markers at each sell order, and hover tooltips.
     - **Realized P/L by symbol** diverging horizontal bar chart centered on `$0` (red bars left for losses, green bars right for gains) with inline `±$ (±%)` labels and hover details.
   - Register **`Agentic Dashboard`** (`viewMode === 'agentic-dashboard'`) directly next to the **Brokers** tab in `frontend/src/app/page.tsx`.
3. **Verification**:
   - Unit test FIFO P/L calculation, win rate, cumulative curve, and per-symbol sorting.
   - Restart FastAPI backend on port `8080` and verify live response from `/api/rh/activity-dashboard?starting_cash=500` and frontend compilation on `http://localhost:3000`.
